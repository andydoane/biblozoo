Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

BibloZoo is designed to keep your information private. The app does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking software in this version.

Information Stored on Your Device

BibloZoo stores information locally so it can remember your family’s progress. This may include Zookeeper profile names and profile pictures, learned verses and memorization progress, game progress and medals, BibloPet unlocks, names and backgrounds, and recent practice activity.

This locally stored app data is not automatically sent to us.

Optional Voice Recording

Flashcards can use the microphone with your permission to let you record and play back your voice. You can also practice without recording. Audio is held only in memory for the current attempt. It is discarded when you continue, re-record, or leave Flashcards. Recordings are not saved to files, profile data, or backups, and are never uploaded.

Photos and Files in Ghost Writer

Ghost Writer can let you choose one picture from Photos or Files to use as a background. The selected picture is processed on your device for the current activity. BibloZoo does not automatically upload it or add it to your saved Zookeeper profile.

If you choose Save or Share, BibloZoo may prepare a temporary picture or silent video file on your device. You can choose whether to save it to Photos or share it using the device's sharing options. Temporary export files are cleaned up when the action finishes. A service you choose to share with may handle your file under its own privacy practices.

Internet Use

The packaged app is designed to use content included with the app. If you use BibloZoo as a website or installed web app, your browser connects to the website host to download BibloZoo and its updates.

The web hosting provider may process standard technical information associated with web requests according to its own privacy practices. BibloZoo itself does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services in this version.

Backups

BibloZoo can export and import a family backup when you choose to use those features. Backup files can include Zookeeper names and memorization progress. Files are created or opened only at your direction. BibloZoo does not automatically upload your backups to a server. If you choose to share a backup, the destination you select handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Deleting Your Data

You can reset a Zookeeper’s progress or erase all locally stored family data from Settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at biblozooapp@gmail.com .
