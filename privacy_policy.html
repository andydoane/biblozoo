Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

BibloZoo is designed to keep your information private. The app does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking software in this version.

Information Stored on Your Device

BibloZoo stores information locally so it can remember your family’s progress. This may include Zookeeper profile names and profile pictures, learned verses and memorization progress, game progress and medals, BibloPet unlocks, names and backgrounds, and recent practice activity.

This locally stored app data is not automatically sent to us.

Internet Use

The packaged app is designed to use content included with the app. If you use BibloZoo as a website or installed web app, your browser connects to the website host to download BibloZoo and its updates.

The web hosting provider may process standard technical information associated with web requests according to its own privacy practices. BibloZoo itself does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services in this version.

Backups

BibloZoo can export and import a family backup when you choose to use those features. Backup files are created or opened only at your direction. BibloZoo does not automatically upload your backups to a server.

Deleting Your Data

You can reset a Zookeeper’s progress or erase all locally stored family data from Settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at biblozooapp@gmail.com .
