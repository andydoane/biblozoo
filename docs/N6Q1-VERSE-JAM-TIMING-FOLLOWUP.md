## Fix inventory (all isolated to Verse Jam audio and its Swift allowlisted sounds)

1. **False task switch at final round:** The gameplay watchdog used a 2.2-second timeout whenever no words/score UI were visible. Legitimate waiting for the last measure and the “that's / the / end” musical sequence could exceed that threshold and invoke `recoverStuckGameplay()`, which explicitly sends plain Welcome Back. **Treat the completed-last-chunk wait and `ending_sequence` phase as legitimate long musical phases** (the existing 8-second threshold), while preserving the watchdog for genuine hangs. No synthesized UIKit events, fake app tasks or automatic session takeover.
2. **Got It! delay:** Full predecoded note, drum, voice and 7×4 BPM/pad combinations were built on the `Got It!` tap. Start preflight synchronously on the real **speed-selection** click, while the Heads Up warning is visible, and retain the pending/built bank across that screen instead of discarding it at navigation. `Got It!` consumes that in-flight preparation; only a **genuine tap** can retry if preparation failed or was interrupted. Prevent the Heads Up button's `pointerdown` UI pop from invalidating the still-building game bank. If the user taps Got It before a cold bank finishes, it must still wait safely rather than play a silent beat; this can remain slower on a first launch.
3. **Missing/late “Time” and “Speed” notes:** Native `scheduleNativeJam` previously woke at the *exact* beat and submitted through JS/Capacitor/main Swift/serial PCM worker, often after painting the word. Submit a **100ms bounded lookahead** (never entire bars in advance) with a near-future `scheduledEpochMs`. Swift validates it only for the 13 fixed Jam IDs, bounds it to approximately −160 to +350ms, recomputes remaining time on its serial worker and schedules `AVAudioPlayerNode` against `AVAudioTime(hostTime:)`. Pre-schedule all three interstitial word notes before entering the UI word timer loop. Keep the original authored first-word chord/phrase, note pitches and durations, BPM/beat count, intro, drums, filler voices, pads, score and browser paths unchanged. Stale generations/timeouts still cancel on pause/menu/exit; absolutely no early background playback.
4. **Excessive bank render/recovery:** Swift Jam note/pad buffer generation called expensive exponential `pow()` millions of times inside each PCM sample loop. Compute exponential attack/decay ratios **once per fixed source recipe/buffer** and multiply gain per frame, preserving essentially the same oscillator shape, sample count and fade envelope. On a still-active menu resume, `prepareFromGesture` rechecks the session without tearing down a healthy warmed bank. Deduplicate native effect teardown when UIKit `.inactive`, `blur`, visibility and shared Welcome Back all report the **same** interruption. A **genuine** background interruption still discards effect buffers and must re-preload after a real Let's Go; no passive priority restoration. Seeing two rotating phrases is evidence of elapsed processing, not two user retry attempts.
5. **“Great!” and final arpeggio:** Original `stopRun()` sent `jam-group` stop that stops **all 12 warm note nodes** immediately before `playCompletionArpeggio()`, potentially delaying/cutting the first notes. New **scoped `jam-groove`** stop only affects drums, pads and five recorded voices and leaves note nodes available for the final C-major arpeggio. Schedule its native initial note with 120ms lead and render the end screen after about **260ms** rather than a blank 900ms; the musical tail can ring while the end screen appears. Web/PWA/Android retain 900ms. Replays/exits still perform the original full bank stop and cannot leave runaway beats.
