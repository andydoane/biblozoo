## Apple App Privacy: provisional answer only, not a submission approval

Apple defines **data “collected”** for its App Privacy labels as data **transmitted off the device** in a way that lets the developer or a third-party partner access it beyond the time needed to service a request; data **processed only on-device** is not collected for these answers. It also requires disclosure of relevant integrated third-party SDK practices, not just developer-written code.

- Official guidance: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- App Store Connect privacy workflow: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy
- Third-party SDK obligations and Xcode aggregate report: https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/
- Device App Privacy Report: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/network/inspecting-app-activity-data

**Likely candidate for native 1.0 after final verification:** `No, we do not collect data from this app` **only if** actual Release-device testing finds no developer/third-party SDK data transmission that Apple counts as collection, and user-initiated OS sharing is correctly understood and scoped. **Do not preselect it yet.** If any SDK, endpoint or uploaded voice/photo/profile data is found, classify the exact data type, purpose, linking and tracking instead. Do not confuse local Zookeeper storage with collection, or use `NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes=[]` as proof of zero collection.
