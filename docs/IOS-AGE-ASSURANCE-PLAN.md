## 2. Evidence and unresolved obligations

- [Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 121](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BC/htm/BC.121.v3.htm): §§121.051–.056 apply to apps made available in Texas through a store, require rating/content disclosures, significant-change notices, use of store age category and minor consent signals, restricted data use and deletion after verification. §121.022(e)(2) places revocation notification duty on the store. No explicit developer-hosted-server mandate identified in these sections; do not infer that notification handling is optional or that every shared-device user can be verified by one Apple Account. No general offline/hobby/Kids/no-IAP exemption identified. §121.053 includes data, rating/content, monetization and material functionality changes. Assess each update rather than treating every patch or none as significant.
- [Apple Texas update June 3](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne): new Texas accounts subject to age/consent changes June 4; checklist includes Declared Age Range, PermissionKit, StoreKit rating property and server notification. [Apple age assurance Q&A](https://developer.apple.com/support/age-assurance/) explains OS prevents launch after revoked parental consent, recommends RESCIND_CONSENT for revocations, requires developer handling of significant changes and age checks where law requires. Existing accounts on iOS/iPadOS 18 or earlier unaffected per Q&A; older OS and future laws still need case-by-case review. OS blocking vs app already running, offline revocation and notification-only obligations are unresolved.
- [Native implementation sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/declaredagerange/implementing-age-assurance-and-permissions) uses Declared Age Range, PermissionKit and iCloud key-value storage for significant-change handling; that demonstrates a native flow, not complete evidence of revocation compliance or a requirement to use iCloud. [Declared Age Range capability](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/declaredagerange/), [PermissionKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/permissionkit/), [Kids tools / StoreKit ageRatingCode](https://developer.apple.com/kids/) and [sandbox scenarios](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/testing-age-assurance-in-sandbox) are primary implementation references. Use current SDK interfaces rather than guessed Swift signatures.
- [November 19 state-law overview](https://www.privacyworld.blog/2026/11/app-store-age-verification-laws-your-questions-answered-updated/) reports Texas enforceable while appeal pending, California distinct device age-signal law and Alabama scheduled Jan 1 2027 (Alabama litigation), Utah May 6 2027 and Louisiana July 1 2027 after amendments. These are secondary timeline findings, not full legal analysis. Confirm current statutes, litigation and Apple applicability at release. Do not use hard-coded state geolocation or assume US storefront selection excludes any state.
