## Decision and boundaries

Do not enable the live gate, request an entitlement, enroll/pay, add a backend (including free tier), add iCloud, collect transaction/account identifiers, or change family data. BibloZoo is planned as US-only, $4.99 paid upfront, no IAP, no external account, no developer server, no ads. Its existing local family profiles are not Apple Accounts. Keep Grown-up Check and offline gameplay unchanged in normal builds. This is a technical evidence assessment, not legal advice or a declaration of compliance. The no-backend constraint remains binding unless owner explicitly changes it. No forum post or support inquiry sent.

Texas law requires store notification of withdrawal (§121.022(e)(2)); §§121.024, .054 require current store age/consent information and developer verification; §121.055 restricts use and requires deletion after verification. These provisions do not expressly prescribe a developer-operated server. The documented Apple transport is server-to-server; lack of an express server mandate does not establish sufficiency of launch blocking. [Apple February 24 Utah/Louisiana announcement](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey) also names the APIs and server notifications, with distinct account dates. Do not generalize Texas to every state; recheck current law, litigation, exemptions, account applicability and other US requirements before distribution. Apple Q&A says review process is unchanged and directs legal questions to counsel. A successful review does not resolve legal compliance.

## Code inspection at merged baseline

`BibloZooAgeStartupCoordinator.evaluate(in:)` calls the staged live assessment only with strict live gate enabled, supplies `.unknown` for initial consent, and considers version 1.0 the staged first release. Regulated minor with unknown consent is held. Fixture decisions are fake, never real approval. Foreground tickets invalidate on background; age recheck is not a revocation signal. `BibloZooAppRatingService.compare(previous:)` has no caller, no baseline storage. Both flags mutually exclusive and Release live compile fence intact. No code changes are proposed here.

## Resolution questions and go/no-go

1. Does a supported native signal explicitly establish current initial permission for an affected child's paid-upfront app, including purchase/download and Family Sharing? Is the platform's enforcement plus age signal sufficient for the developer verification duty? Need authoritative Apple technical explanation and appropriate legal evaluation; no assumption.
2. Is RESCIND_CONSENT server reception required for a local-only paid app without IAP, accounts or remotely provided service? If so, what action is expected when OS blocks access and local data cannot be reached from server? Can a native-only app satisfy the obligation? Need affirmative support, not silence or optional URL label.
3. What happens on live foreground revocation, suspended/resumed app, offline first/repeat launch, device/account/storefront change and existing accounts/older OS? Test actual behavior separately from sandbox notification emulation. No local deletion of family content triggered by speculation.
4. What supported source and scope for rating baseline and version-specific approved updates works without iCloud or persistent personal data? Do not confuse app metadata with a user's age or consent. Review cross-device/reinstall/backup behavior before implementing.

**Next decision:** do not keep adding speculative native policy. Owner previously chose implement/test then App Review rather than forum. Preserve that choice: no outreach on owner's behalf. Offer owner a narrowly scoped Apple Developer Support inquiry (non-technical) or code-level forum/Feedback Assistant if they elect to change approach; legal interpretation requires counsel. Alternatively owner may explicitly approve enrollment for isolated sandbox evidence and continue unrelated release prep while live activation remains blocked. Neither choice resolves the gap alone. If confirmed mandatory developer backend or unacceptable offline loss, stop and reassess architecture/distribution with owner. No charge or launch by default.
