Troubleshooting

App won't respond

Close BibloZoo and open it again. If needed, restart your device. Do not delete or reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step: Zookeeper profiles and progress are stored locally, and uninstalling may erase them.

Sound or microphone trouble

Check your device's volume and audio output, including any connected Bluetooth device. Flashcards voice recording is optional. If recording is unavailable, check the microphone permission for BibloZoo in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. You can still practice a verse without recording.

Ghost Writer photos and videos

To import a background, select a supported picture from Photos or Files when prompted. For saving a Ghost Writer picture or silent video to Photos, check BibloZoo's Photos permission in iOS Settings. Sharing is optional and uses the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Family backups

In BibloZoo, open Settings > Backup & Restore > Export Family Backup to make a copy of saved family profile and verse progress data. Keep the exported JSON file somewhere private, such as Files, and maintain a second safe copy.

Be careful with Import Backup: depending on the option selected, it can replace existing Zookeepers. A family backup does not necessarily include every separate Zoo rescue or surprise statistic. Verify your backup before making changes, and keep the existing app installed if you need to preserve its data.
